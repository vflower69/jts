Quick Summary

JimothyTracker.Org documents Jimothy’s movement patterns for educational and ecological purposes. The data must never be used to locate, chase, disturb, or interact with Jimothy. All submissions must be accurate, honest, and respectful of wildlife ethics. Violations will result in removal, restriction, or escalation to authorities.

🐾🐾🐾

Purpose of JimothyTracker.Org

JimothyTracker.Org is a community wildlife awareness and ecological documentation project. Its purpose is to support responsible observation of Jimothy’s natural movement patterns, habitat preferences, and seasonal behavior. Sightings submitted by the community help build a reliable dataset used exclusively for educational, ecological, and conservation‑related purposes.

This platform is not designed, intended, or permitted to be used for locating Jimothy in real time, approaching him, or attempting any form of interaction. All data is provided strictly for passive observation and community education.

🐾🐾🐾

Wildlife Ethics Statement

Jimothy is a wild animal protected under local wildlife regulations. Users of JimothyTracker.Org must adhere to the following ethical principles:

  • Do not approach, pursue, or attempt to interact with Jimothy.
  • Do not feed, lure, or alter his behavior in any way.
  • Do not trespass or enter restricted areas to locate him.
  • Do not encourage others to physically seek him out.
  • Do not use tracking data for any activity that could disturb or endanger wildlife.
🐾🐾🐾

Prohibited Uses

JimothyTracker.Org must NOT be used for:

  • Hunting, capturing, or attempting to capture Jimothy
  • Chasing, approaching, or stalking him
  • Coordinating group attempts to locate him
  • Trying to lure him out or interfere with his routine
  • Trespassing or entering unsafe areas to “find” him
  • Posting intentionally false or fabricated sightings
  • Submitting locations outside his realistic range

Reports that are clearly unrealistic or harmful will be removed to protect the integrity of the dataset and Jimothy’s safety.

🐾🐾🐾

Data Accuracy Policy

To maintain a scientifically useful and trustworthy dataset, all submitted sightings must meet the following standards:

  • Locations must reflect where Jimothy was actually observed.
  • Submissions must be honest and non‑fabricated.
  • Reports must be specific and verifiable when possible.
  • No guesses, assumptions, or second‑hand rumors.
  • No duplicate submissions for the same sighting event.
🐾🐾🐾

Community Enforcement Policy

All submissions are subject to moderation. Reports may be edited, flagged, or removed at any time. Users who violate wildlife ethics or data policies may be restricted or permanently removed from submitting sightings.

Severe or repeated violations will result in permanent removal of reporting privileges and may be escalated to relevant wildlife authorities if behavior poses a risk.

🐾🐾🐾

Consequences for Misuse

  • Removal of harmful or policy‑violating submissions
  • Temporary or permanent suspension of reporting privileges
  • IP‑based restrictions to prevent further misuse
  • Notification to wildlife authorities if behavior poses a risk
  • Legal action in cases involving harassment, stalking, trespassing, or attempts to harm wildlife
🐾🐾🐾

Jimothy Safety Tips

To ensure Jimothy’s safety and the safety of the community, please follow these guidelines:

  • Observe Jimothy from a distance — never approach.
  • Keep pets indoors or leashed when Jimothy is nearby.
  • Secure trash bins to avoid attracting raccoons.
  • Never feed Jimothy or leave food outside intentionally.
  • Report any injured wildlife to local animal control.
