A Community Field Journal

Jimothy is a shy, clever raccoon who roams the neighborhoods and parks of Seattle. This public tracker helps neighbors share respectful sightings and keep an eye on his little adventures. Please see the About page for more information.

Please observe wildlife from a distance. Jimothy is not domesticated and should never be approached. Do not feed, corner, or follow Jimothy.

Seattle’s Little Wanderer

Jimothy the raccoon is a uniquely proportioned Seattle local, often spotted around the Ballard. His short neck, rounded torso, and long legs caught public attention after a video of him roaming outdoors went viral in mid‑July 2026. Since then, neighbors have reported frequent sightings of Jimothy exploring the Ballard area and its surrounding green spaces.
